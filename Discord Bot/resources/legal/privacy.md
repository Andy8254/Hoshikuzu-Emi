# Privacy Policy — Hoshikuzu Emi

*Last updated: April 30, 2026*

---

## 1. Data Collection

Hoshikuzu Emi collects and stores only the minimum data necessary to provide its features:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Server (Guild) IDs
* Linked external usernames (e.g., TETR.IO usernames)
* Server configuration data (such as role IDs)

No sensitive personal data (such as email addresses, passwords, or private messages) is collected.

---

## 2. Purpose of Data Use

The collected data is used solely for:

* Linking Discord users to external accounts
* Providing player-related features (e.g., `/tetrio`)
* Managing server-specific configurations and permissions

---

## 3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the machine hosting the bot (e.g., the developer’s PC or server).
No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Retention

Data is retained as long as necessary for the bot’s functionality.
Users may request deletion of their stored data at any time.

---

## 5. User Rights

Users have the right to:

* Request access to their stored data
* Request deletion of their data

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

The bot retrieves public data from third-party services such as:

* TETR.IO (via TETRA CHANNEL API)

These services may have their own privacy policies.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the bot evolves. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance of any changes.

---

## 8. Contact

For questions or data requests, contact `@andy8254` on Discord.
