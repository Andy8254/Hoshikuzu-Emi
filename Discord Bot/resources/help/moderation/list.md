# Moderation Commands
Manual moderation and case logging commands.
## /mod warn
Record a warning.
## /mod note
Record a staff note.
## /mod history
Show recent moderation history.
## /mod timeout
Timeout a user.
## /mod clear_timeout
Clear a timeout.
## /mod kick
Kick a user.
## /mod ban
Ban a user.
## /mod unban
Unban a user.
