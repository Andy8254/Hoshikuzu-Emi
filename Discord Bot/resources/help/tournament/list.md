# Tournament Commands
Tournament creation, registration, check-in, seeding, calls for staff, and configuration.
## /tournament create
Create a tournament.
## /tournament edit
Edit tournament name, platform, format, or status.
## /tournament delete
Delete one tournament.
## /tournament clear
Expunge tournament module data.
## /tournament info
Show public tournament information.
## /tournament staff_info
Show staff-tailored tournament information.
## /tournament registration_open
Open tournament registration.
## /tournament registration_close
Close tournament registration.
## /tournament checkin_open
Open tournament check-in.
## /tournament checkin_close
Close tournament check-in.
## /tournament register
Register, unregister, or staff-register a player.
## /tournament checkin
Check in, undo check-in, or staff-handle check-in.
## /tournament call_staff
Call staff for a match.
## /tournament participants
List tournament participants.
## /tournament seed
Seed checked-in players.
## /tournament config
Configure tournament channels, roles, format, and rulesets.
## /tournament bracket
Manage bracket generation, matches, streams, forfeits, no-shows, threads, and SVG exports.
