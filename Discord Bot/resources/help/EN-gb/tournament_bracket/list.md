# /tournament bracket
Bracket, match, stream, no-show, score reporting, standings, and visual export tools.

## Who Uses This
Tournament staff. Player-facing match actions are exposed through buttons after threads are created.

## Workflow
Generate from checked-in players, inspect current matches, create threads, then report/correct results.

## Commands
- `generate`: Generates bracket matches from checked-in players.
- `current`: Shows current playable matches.
- `round`: Shows matches in one round.
- `match`: Shows one match.
- `report`: Reports a match score.
- `correct_report`: Corrects a completed match report.
- `forfeit`: Records a player forfeit or DQ.
- `resolve_no_shows`: Resolves due match no-shows.
- `threads`: Creates Discord match threads.
- `stream_assign`: Marks a match as assigned to stream.
- `stream_clear`: Clears a stream assignment.
- `stream_list`: Shows streamed matches.
- `standings`: Shows Swiss or round-robin standings.
- `svg`: Exports the full bracket as SVG.
- `match_svg`: Exports one match as SVG.
