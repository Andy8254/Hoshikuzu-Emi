# /profile
Personal player profile tools for tournament identity, linked platform accounts, language preference, and TETR.IO lookup.

## Who Uses This
Players first; staff can use lookup pages when helping registration or resolving identity questions.

## Workflow
Run `/profile init`, link accounts with `/profile link`, then use tournament panels during events.

## Commands
- `init`: Creates your local tournament profile.
- `link`: Links a platform account to your profile.
- `unlink`: Removes one linked platform account.
- `show`: Shows a player profile.
- `delete`: Deletes your local profile data.
- `language`: Sets your personal display language.
- `tetrio`: Looks up a TETR.IO profile.

## Help
Use `/bot help category:profile command:<command>` for a command-specific page.
