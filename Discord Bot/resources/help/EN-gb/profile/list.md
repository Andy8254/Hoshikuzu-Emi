# /profile
Personal user profile tools for tournament identity, linked platform accounts, language preference, and TETR.IO lookup.
## Who Uses This
Mainly regular users and players.
Staff can use lookup pages when helping with registration or resolving identity questions.
## Workflow
Run `/profile init`, link accounts with `/profile link`, then use tournament panels during events.
## Commands
- `init`: Creates your local tournament profile.
- `link`: Links your platform account to your Discord profile.
- `unlink`: Removes one platform account linked to you.
- `show`: Shows your current player profile.
- `delete`: Deletes your local profile data.
- `language`: Sets your UI display language.
- `tetrio`: Looks up the selected user's TETR.IO profile.
## Help
Use `/bot help category:profile command:<command>` for a command-specific page.
