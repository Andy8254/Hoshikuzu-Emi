# /profile link
Links the selected platform account to your profile.
## Usage
`/profile link platform:<platform> id:<username>`
## Details
Connects a username from TETR.IO, Jstris, PPT2, TEC, Tetra eSports, Classic Tetris, or another supported platform to your Discord profile.
## Notes
Enter the public in-game username or ID staff should recognize.
Do not intentionally enter another person's username. If staff confirms intentional impersonation, it may result in server-level moderation action.
